Bruschetta

Ingredients:

  • 1 baguette, sliced
  • 2 cups diced tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Balsamic glaze (optional)

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Arrange baguette slices on a baking sheet and brush with olive oil.
  3. Toast bread slices for about 5-7 minutes until golden.
  4. In a bowl, mix tomatoes, garlic, basil, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  5. Spoon the tomato mixture onto toasted bread slices.
  6. Drizzle with balsamic glaze if desired and serve immediately.

Stuffed Mushrooms

Ingredients:

  • 12 large button mushrooms
  • 1/2 cup cream cheese, softened
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Olive oil for drizzling

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Remove stems from mushrooms and chop stems finely.
  3. In a bowl, mix cream cheese, Parmesan, garlic, parsley, salt, pepper, and chopped mushroom stems.
  4. Stuff each mushroom cap with the cheese mixture.
  5. Place mushrooms on a baking sheet and drizzle with olive oil.
  6. Bake for 20 minutes until mushrooms are tender and tops are golden.
  7. Serve warm.
